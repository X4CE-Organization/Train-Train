# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026

Train Train / 混乱火车 first playable release.

- Eight low-poly regions and 48 campaign levels
- Campaign, endless and level editor modes
- Immediate train departure with click-to-stop dispatch control
- Four-color passengers, mixed-color cargo capacity and multi-car trains
- Layered ground track, elevated bridges, ramps, tunnels, sidings and uncontrolled crossings
- Closed switch arrows, route indication, collision feedback and time-based ratings
- Four train styles, garage progression, local saves and best-score tracking
- Drag-to-draw level editor with stations, trains, undo/redo, validation and JSON import/export
- Runtime-generated low-poly models, scenery, interface and audio
- Windows x86_64 and macOS universal builds
- MIT-licensed source with automated project validation
